What they are

Yitz3 uploads, Yitz4 uploads, Yitz5 uploads, Yitz6 uploads and Yitz7 uploads are copies of one command-line program that runs on the computer of the channel owner, Yitzchak Shmalo. Each copy belongs to one of his YouTube channels. It uploads his documentaries, lectures, poems and short clips to that channel and then:

  • sets each video's thumbnail,
  • adds the caption track made from the narration,
  • places the video in its playlists,
  • sets or corrects its title, description and tags.

YouTube API Services

The Channel Uploaders use YouTube API Services. By using them you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and these tools' handling of it in our privacy policy.

Who can use them

No one else. The tools are not offered to the public, have no sign-up and no web interface, and each acts only on the channel that signs in to it. They read no other channel's data.

Sign-in and removing access

Each tool signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0). Google shows a consent screen listing the permissions requested, and the tool can act on a channel only after its owner approves. Access can be removed at any time on the Google Account permissions page, security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, by choosing the tool and removing its access.
