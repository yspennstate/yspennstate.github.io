Tadpole Film Uploader

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

Tadpole Film Uploader ("the tool") is a private command-line program run by Yitzchak Shmalo on his own computer to upload and manage videos on his own YouTube channel, UCsqMyA2aeE-GF_JqE_x_sHg. This policy explains what information the tool uses and how.

YouTube API Services and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Information Google receives when you sign in and use YouTube is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What information the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, Google's consent screen asks him to allow the tool to "see, edit, and permanently delete your YouTube videos, ratings, comments and captions" and to "view YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content". With that approval the tool accesses, on the owner's own channel only:

  • the channel's ID and name, to confirm it is acting on the right channel;
  • the channel's own videos, their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and processing status;
  • the channel's caption tracks;
  • the channel's playlists and the videos in them;
  • the YouTube Analytics reports of the channel's own videos.

It does not access any other channel's data.

What information is stored

  • Sign-in tokens. The access and refresh tokens Google issues are saved in a file on the owner's computer so the tool can keep working without signing in each time.
  • A record of the tool's own uploads. For each video it uploads: the video ID, title, the local file name, the playlists it was added to and the date. This is kept on the owner's computer to manage his own channel.

Nothing is stored on any server run by us. The tool has no database, no website accounts and no user list.

How the information is used and shared

The information is used only to upload the owner's videos and to set their thumbnails, captions, playlists, titles and descriptions. Analytics reports, where read, are used only by the owner to review his own channel. It is sent only between the owner's computer and Google, through YouTube API Services. It is not shared with, sold to or disclosed to anyone else.

Advertising, cookies and tracking

The tool shows no advertising and lets no third party serve content. Neither the tool nor these web pages use cookies, analytics or any other tracking technology on your device.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove the tool's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Once access is removed the tool can no longer act on the channel. Stored tokens and records are deleted within 30 days after access is removed, or within 7 days of a request to delete them.
